Set up a language voice

Read Aloud only speaks with a voice that matches the language you chose. This prevents the wrong-language accent problem.

Windows 10 or 11

  1. Open Settings and search for Speech.
  2. Choose Manage voices, then Add voices.
  3. Install the language you selected in Under Progress.
  4. Close and reopen the browser, then try Read Aloud again.

Mac, Chromebook, or another device

Install or enable a text-to-speech voice for your chosen language in your device accessibility or speech settings, then restart the browser. Under Progress will use it only when its language matches your selection.
